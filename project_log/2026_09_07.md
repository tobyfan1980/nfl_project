## Correlation Exercises (pooled 2022-2025)

- 2025 histogram of home minus away `travel_miles_log` split by winner:
  both distributions peak in [-1, 0); mean is -1.77 for home wins and
  -2.54 for away wins.
- `travel_or_not`: away team traveled in essentially every game
  (768 of 770), so it carries no signal. Home team `travel_or_not` is
  weakly associated with home wins (Phi = -0.021, chi2 p = 0.55).
- `rest_period`: no significant effect from either side individually.
  The rest-advantage tabulation gives home-win% 57.4% when home is more
  rested, 57.2% when equal, and 48.7% when the away team is more rested
  (chi2 p = 0.24). Directionally consistent with rest helping the visitor,
  but not significant.
- `timezone_difference`: no significant effect
  (chi2 p ~= 0.54 home, 0.58 away, 0.64 tz-crossing advantage).
- `opponent_last5_win_rate` (schedule strength gap): significant.
  Point-biserial r = -0.157 (p < 0.0001). Tertile home-win%: 62.9%
  when the away team had the tougher recent schedule, 58.2% when
  similar, 45.5% when the home team had the tougher schedule.
  Reflects schedule-strength adjustment picking up unobserved team
  quality more than a causal schedule effect.

## Prediction Models (train 2022-2024, hold out 2025)

- Chosen feature: X = home_last5_win_rate - away_last5_win_rate.
- OLS: Y_hat = 0.5735 + 0.4156 X. Slope t = 7.69, p ~ 1e-14. R^2 = 0.093.
- Logistic: logit P = 0.3271 + 1.8526 X. Slope z = 7.03, p ~ 2e-12.
  McFadden R^2 = 0.071.
- Both models' 0.5 decision boundary sits at X ~ -0.177, so at classification
  time they predict the same games identically.
- 2025 hold-out accuracy (weeks 6-18, 194 games): 69.6% for both models;
  baseline "always home" 53.1%. Restricted to weeks 10-18 (137 games):
  72.3% for both; baseline 53.3%. Per-week accuracy peaked at Weeks 11
  and 16 (~87%) and dipped to 56% at Week 18 (starter rest before
  the playoffs).
- Tried adding rest as a covariate. With both `rest_less` and `rest_more`
  dummies (baseline `rest_same`), only `rest_less` was significant
  (OLS coef -0.122, p = 0.033; logistic OR = 0.58). Refit without
  `rest_more`: coefficients stable, in-sample R^2 unchanged, but 2025
  accuracy dropped to 65.5% / 65.7%. Reverted to the single-feature
  win-rate model as the working baseline.
