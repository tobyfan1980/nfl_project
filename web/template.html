Each Tuesday night the picks for the coming week are saved to a file and never changed. This list grades those saved picks against the final scores, so the record above is exactly what we called ahead of time.

How the picks work

1

Check the streak

For each team, count wins in its last five games this season. A tie counts as half a win. Five wins in five is a 100% streak.

2

Compare

Subtract the visitor's streak from the home team's. A big positive gap favors the home team, and a big negative gap favors the visitor.

3

Turn it into odds

A logistic curve fit on the 2022–2024 seasons turns that gap into a home win chance. Whoever is above 50% is the pick.
