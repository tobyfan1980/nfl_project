About PocketPicks

What the model is

PocketPicks turns each NFL game into a win probability. The model is a logistic regression trained on past seasons. Its main signal is the difference between the two teams' win rates over their last five games. A team coming off a hot streak gets a higher probability; a team in a slump gets a lower one.

On a held-out set of games the model never saw during training, it picked the winner about 70% of the time. That is a useful baseline, not a guarantee for any single game.

Not betting advice

PocketPicks is an educational project. The predictions are statistical estimates and can be wrong, and they do not account for injuries, weather, or anything else outside recent form. Not betting advice. Please do not wager money based on this site.
